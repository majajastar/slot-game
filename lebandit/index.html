🌊 Cascading Reels

1. Winning symbols are removed

2. Symbols drop down to fill gaps

3. New symbols appear at top

4. New wins are calculated!

5. Continues until no more wins

✨ Golden Squares

When symbols are removed, they leave golden squares!

Golden squares persist through cascades.

🌈 Rainbow Feature

After cascade ends, chance for Rainbow to appear!

When Rainbow appears:

  • Golden squares become Coins, Clovers, or Pots of Gold
  • Coins: Bronze (0.2x-4x), Silver (5x-20x), Gold (25x-500x)
  • Clovers: Multiply adjacent coins/pots (2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 100x)
  • Pot of Gold: Collects ALL coin values!

If Pots appear, golden squares refill for another round!
